OWNER / OPERATORS: REHEARSAL DISPLAY
====================================

Chords, sections, and lyrics on a screen instead of paper.
No internet needed. Nothing to install.

START
-----
1. Double-click index.html. It opens in Safari (Chrome works too).
2. Pick a song: arrow keys up/down, then Enter.
3. Press F for fullscreen.

Tip: drag index.html into the right-hand side of the Dock (next to the Trash)
so it is one click next time.

DURING A SONG
-------------
  Right arrow, Down, Space, Enter, PageDown ...... next line
  Left arrow, Up, PageUp, Backspace .............. back a line
  ]  or  Shift + Right ........................... next section
  [  or  Shift + Left ............................ back a section (or top of this one)
  Home  or  R .................................... back to bar 1
  S  or  Esc ..................................... song list
  F .............................................. fullscreen on/off

  Mouse: click the right side of the screen = next, left side = back.
  A wireless mouse works as a hand-held page turner.

The yellow box is where you are. The top strip is the whole song map.
"NEXT" at the bottom shows the next section's chords before you get there.

TEMPO MODE (play along at the song's tempo)
------------------------------------------
  P ................ start/stop. One bar of count-in clicks, then it advances
                     bar by bar by itself. The lit yellow bar is "now".
  C ................ metronome click on/off
  + / - ............ tempo up/down 1 bpm (remembered per song)
  Arrows / ] [ ..... jump while it runs; it keeps time from the new spot

FOOT PEDAL (when we get one)
---------------------------
Most Bluetooth page-turner pedals act like a keyboard, sending arrow keys or
PageUp/PageDown, so they should just work: pair it in System Settings >
Bluetooth, then step on it.

To check what a pedal sends, press K. Step on the pedal and the panel shows the
key and what it does. If it says "no action", the key name goes in config.js
(instructions are at the top of that file). If you edit it in TextEdit, first
turn OFF Edit > Substitutions > Smart Quotes, or the curly quotes it inserts
will break the app. Then reload the page.

MIDI footswitches only work in Chrome (press K, then M).

KEEP THE SCREEN ON
------------------
If the display goes to sleep mid-song: System Settings > Lock Screen >
"Turn display off when inactive" > Never. Or open Terminal, type
caffeinate -d and leave that window open while you play.
